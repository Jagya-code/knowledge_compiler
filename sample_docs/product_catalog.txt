INSURANCE PRODUCT CATALOG

Introduction

This document provides an overview of the company's insurance product portfolio, including product lines, coverage structures, and target market segments.

PERSONAL LINES PRODUCTS

Motor Insurance

The motor insurance product range covers private passenger vehicles, motorcycles, and light commercial vehicles. Coverage options include third-party liability, comprehensive, and collision. Key product features include accident forgiveness, new-for-old replacement for vehicles under three years, and roadside assistance.

Home and Contents Insurance

The home insurance product provides building and contents coverage for owner-occupiers and landlords. Coverage extensions include accidental damage, legal expenses, and home emergency assistance. Sum insured adequacy tools are integrated into the digital sales journey.

COMMERCIAL LINES PRODUCTS

Commercial Property Insurance

Commercial property products cover buildings, plant, machinery, and stock for businesses ranging from small retailers to large industrial operations. Policy structures include standard perils, all-risks, and business interruption extensions.

Product Development Process

New products and product modifications follow a structured development process including market research, actuarial pricing, regulatory review, and systems implementation. Product approval requires sign-off from the Chief Underwriting Officer and Legal & Compliance teams.
